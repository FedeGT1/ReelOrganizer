# {{title}}

> One sentence: what a reader understands after this page that they did not before.

## The problem

Start from the constraint, not the solution. What does the world impose here — a phone
line that cannot pause, a session usable once, a model charged by the token? An
explainer earns its length by answering **why it is like this**. If it only lists what
exists, it should have been a component page.

## How it works

Narrative, not bullets. Name real modules inline as backticked repo-relative paths.
Never wikilink a code path.

## The parts that surprise people

The non-obvious mechanics — anything a reader would get wrong by guessing.

## Trade-offs and what was rejected

What the alternative would have cost. Usually the most valuable section, and the one
most often missing from the source material.
